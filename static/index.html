How it works

  1. Data: 2 years of hourly footfall for 8 stations (112k rows) plus an event calendar of 66 events (cricket, concerts, Navratri, riverfront). The data is synthetic, generated from commute patterns and event crowd curves.
  2. Features: station, hour, weekday, month, holiday, event type, expected metro riders at the station, minutes to event start, minutes after event end.
  3. Model: Gradient-Boosted Trees (scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor) predict footfall per station per hour. Train/test split is by time: the last 3 months are held out.
  4. Rush detection: forecast ÷ station hourly capacity → low / moderate / high / critical.
  5. Planner: 1-minute simulation of the egress surge. It picks the train headway, opens gates when arrivals exceed gate throughput, meters entry when the paid area passes 85%, and activates holding zones as the street queue grows.
  6. Output: an action timeline plus announcement scripts in Gujarati, Hindi and English (with text-to-speech).
